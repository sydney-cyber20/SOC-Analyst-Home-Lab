Cybersecurity Risk Register


Company Name:
Sydney Cyber Lab


Risk 1:
Weak passwords


Asset Affected:
User accounts


Impact:
Unauthorized access to systems or accounts


Likelihood:
High


Severity:
High


Recommended Control:
Use strong password rules and enable multi-factor authentication.




Risk 2:
Repeated failed login attempts


Asset Affected:
Windows endpoints and user accounts


Impact:
Possible brute-force attack or password guessing


Likelihood:
Medium


Severity:
Medium


Recommended Control:
Enable account lockout policy and monitor Event ID 4625.




Risk 3:
Phishing emails


Asset Affected:
Email accounts and users


Impact:
Credential theft, malware infection, or account compromise


Likelihood:
High


Severity:
High


Recommended Control:
Train users to identify phishing emails and report suspicious messages.




Risk 4:
Unused or disabled accounts not reviewed


Asset Affected:
User accounts


Impact:
Old accounts may be abused by attackers


Likelihood:
Medium


Severity:
Medium


Recommended Control:
Review user accounts regularly and disable accounts that are no longer needed.




Risk 5:
Lack of MFA


Asset Affected:
User accounts and administrator accounts


Impact:
Accounts are easier to compromise if passwords are stolen


Likelihood:
High


Severity:
High


Recommended Control:
Enable multi-factor authentication for all important accounts.




Risk 6:
Poor log monitoring


Asset Affected:
Security logs and endpoints


Impact:
Security incidents may go undetected


Likelihood:
Medium


Severity:
High


Recommended Control:
Monitor Windows Event Logs and SIEM alerts regularly.




Risk 7:
Unauthorized access


Asset Affected:
Business data and systems


Impact:
Data theft, system misuse, or business disruption


Likelihood:
Medium


Severity:
High


Recommended Control:
Apply least privilege access and review permissions regularly.
