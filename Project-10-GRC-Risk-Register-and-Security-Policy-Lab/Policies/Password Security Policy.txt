Password Security Policy


Company Name:
Sydney Cyber Lab


Policy Purpose:
The purpose of this policy is to protect user accounts, administrator accounts, systems, and business data from unauthorized access caused by weak or stolen passwords.


Policy Scope:
This policy applies to all users, administrators, endpoints, and business systems used by Sydney Cyber Lab.


Password Requirements:
1. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
2. Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
3. Users must not reuse old passwords.
4. Users must not share passwords with anyone.
5. Passwords must not be written down in unsecured locations.
6. Default passwords must be changed immediately.
7. Administrator accounts must use stronger passwords than standard user accounts.


Multi-Factor Authentication:
MFA should be enabled for administrator accounts, email accounts, and other important business systems where possible.


Account Lockout:
Accounts should be locked after repeated failed login attempts to reduce the risk of brute-force attacks.


Password Reset:
Password resets must only be performed after confirming the identity of the user requesting the reset.


User Responsibility:
Users are responsible for protecting their passwords and reporting suspected account compromise immediately.


Monitoring:
Repeated failed login attempts should be monitored using Windows Security Logs, Event ID 4625, and SIEM alerts.


Policy Review:
This policy should be reviewed regularly to ensure it remains effective.


Conclusion:
Strong password controls help reduce the risk of unauthorized access, brute-force attacks, and account compromise.
